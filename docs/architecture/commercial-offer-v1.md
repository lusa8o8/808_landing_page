# 808 Digital Systems commercial offer v1

- Status: approved commercial direction; implementation wording pending
- Approved: 2026-08-21
- Scope: new 808 website projects and their first year of service
- Audience: internal product, sales, proposal, and website-copy work

## Commercial position

808 competes on quality, clarity, and the ability to support each client well. The offer is not designed to maximise client volume through low pricing.

Every website tier includes SnapBook, hosting, and routine maintenance for the first 365 days at no additional cost. SnapBook does not currently have a standalone price, and 808 does not currently sell a separate ongoing-care plan.

The internal reasoning used to evaluate future pricing is confidential. Public material must describe what the client receives and the applicable terms without referring to behavioural-data collection, pricing experiments, or internal learning objectives.

## Approved pricing direction

| Tier | One-time project price | Position |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Starter | K12,000 | A focused, high-quality launch for a single service business. |
| Growth | K18,000-K25,000 | A broader service structure and a more tailored customer journey. |
| Multi-Location | K30,000-K40,000+ | Location-aware experiences and the additional complexity of serving multiple branches. |

These figures establish the commercial range. Each proposal must still define the exact pages, content responsibilities, booking configuration, integrations, revision limits, launch requirements, and delivery schedule before work begins.

## Included in every tier for the first 365 days

- SnapBook access and configuration appropriate to the agreed project scope.
- Website hosting.
- Routine maintenance for the delivered website and agreed SnapBook configuration.

The 365-day period must have one explicit start date in the client proposal. Until a different rule is approved, the proposal must state the applicable date rather than relying on an implied definition.

## Routine maintenance boundary

Routine maintenance includes:

- reliability and security updates reasonably required to keep the delivered system operating;
- bug fixes where the delivered implementation does not behave as agreed;
- hosting-related operational work; and
- reasonable corrections to existing business details or supplied content.

Routine maintenance does not include:

- new pages, services, workflows, or substantial features;
- redesigns or material changes to the approved structure;
- custom third-party integrations or data migrations;
- substantial content creation; or
- paid third-party products, dedicated infrastructure, domain registration, or domain renewal unless the proposal explicitly includes them.

Work outside routine maintenance must be scoped and approved separately before it begins.

## End-of-year terms

- There is no automatic paid renewal.
- No year-two price is currently promised or published.
- 808 will present continuation options and their terms before the included period ends.
- The client may accept or decline those options.
- Any operational consequences of declining continuation must be described in the proposal or service agreement; they must not be improvised at renewal time.
- The client should own or control their domain. Any exception must be documented explicitly.

## Public wording rule

The following sentence is approved as the baseline public claim:

> Every 808 website includes SnapBook, hosting and routine maintenance for the first 365 days at no additional cost.

Public copy must not claim that these services are free forever, imply unlimited maintenance, invent a future renewal price, or disclose the confidential internal rationale.

## Decisions required before full website publication

- Exact deliverables and limits for each tier.
- Project payment milestones.
- The standard event that starts the 365-day period.
- Response-time expectations and practical limits for content corrections.
- Continuation, export, suspension, and data-handling terms if a client declines a later offer.
- Treatment of taxes and separately approved third-party charges.

Pricing may be discussed in proposals using the approved ranges now. Full tier cards or detailed price comparisons on the public website should wait until the corresponding deliverables and terms above are settled.
